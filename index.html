How a project runs

Proof, cast, press, bind.

  1. 01 — Proof

    Discovery

    We study the market, the competitors, and the brief until we know exactly what the mark has to do before we design anything.

  2. 02 — Cast

    Design

    We design the system — type, colour, layout, motion — and test it against real use cases before any of it is final.

  3. 03 — Press

    Build

    We build it, whether that's a site, a packaging run, or a full brand book, to a production-grade standard.

  4. 04 — Bind

    Launch

    We ship it, document it, and hand over everything your team needs to run with it on their own.
